Cast to the TV

Casting sends pictures of the board from this computer to a Chromecast or Google TV, so this computer stays on while it casts. A stick or a small computer plugged into the TV runs the board by itself; the TV guide has both.

One click from this button needs the board on https://: put DEPARTUREBOARD_TLS=self-signed in .env and restart, then open the settings page at its https:// address in Chrome or Edge. The button then opens the TV picker.

Right now, with no change: in Chrome, open the board, then ⋮ → Cast… → Cast tab and pick the TV.
