          Cast to the TV

          Casting sends pictures of the board from this computer to a Chromecast or Google TV, so this computer stays on while it casts. A stick or a small computer plugged into the TV runs the board by itself; the TV guide has both.

          One click from this button needs this page in a secure context in Chrome or Edge: either the board on https:// (put DEPARTUREBOARD_TLS=self-signed in .env, restart, open the settings page at its https:// address), or, on the computer running the server, a *.localhost name such as http://dboard.localhost:8080/settings (DEPARTUREBOARD_HOST). The button then opens the TV picker.

          Right now, with no change: in Chrome, open the board, then ⋮ → Cast… → Cast tab and pick the TV.
